🏋️ Logan’s Exercise & PT Center

Gentle movement for His Majesty — comfort first, always. 👑🐾

🛑 Stop the session if Logan cries out, pants unusually, trembles, resists, suddenly weakens, repeatedly knuckles/drags the foot, or seems more painful afterward. These are gentle home activities, not a substitute for a veterinary rehabilitation plan. On a rough day, rest wins.
💙 Logan rule: Use a non-slip surface and his support harness whenever he needs it. Start tiny. The goal is comfortable, controlled movement — not tiring him out. His RIGHT REAR leg gets extra attention, but never force it or pull on the leg.

🧍 Supported Standing

Start: about 10–20 seconds × 2–3 comfortable tries
🎯 RIGHT REAR LEG
  1. Place him on a non-slip surface.
  2. Use the harness to take only enough weight off his rear to keep him steady.
  3. Let all four paws contact the floor as comfortably as possible.
  4. Finish before his legs fatigue or buckle.
💡 Think “help him balance,” not “hold him up completely.” If he can safely bear a little weight, that is the exercise.

↔️ Gentle Weight Shifts

Start: 3–5 tiny shifts each direction
🎯 RIGHT REAR LEG
  1. Begin in a comfortable supported stand.
  2. With one hand at his trunk/hips and the harness ready, gently encourage his body a tiny amount left, then right.
  3. Move only far enough that he adjusts his balance — never shove or make him stumble.
💡 His right side is weak, so keep the movement especially small when shifting weight toward that leg.

🐾 Harness-Assisted Walking

Start: a very short, slow walk — quality over distance
🎯 RIGHT REAR LEG
  1. Fit his rear-support harness comfortably.
  2. Walk on a flat, non-slip route at Logan’s pace.
  3. Support enough to prevent falling, while allowing him to place and use the hind feet when he can.
  4. Turn around before he looks tired.
💡 Several tiny walks can be kinder than one long walk. Avoid stairs, slick floors, sharp turns and “pushing through” fatigue.

🛌 Gentle RIGHT REAR-Leg Range of Motion

Only when relaxed: 3–5 slow, comfortable cycles
🎯 RIGHT REAR LEG
  1. Let Logan lie comfortably on his side.
  2. Support his RIGHT REAR leg with one hand above and one below the joint area.
  3. Slowly bend the leg into a comfortable tucked position, then gently return it toward straight.
  4. Stay completely within his easy range; never stretch against resistance.
💡 This is gentle motion, not a stretch contest. Skip it if handling the hip/leg bothers him or if his vet has told you to restrict joint movement.
